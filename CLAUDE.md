\# GeneriQ — Global Medicine Price Comparison



\## Mission

Show anyone in the world where they can get 

the exact same medicine with identical 

ingredients for the lowest price on the planet.



\## Tagline

Stop overpaying for your health.



\## The Problem We Solve

The same pill, same factory, same ingredients 

can cost 2,682% more in the USA than India.

A mother in Kenya pays half her weekly wage 

for antibiotics that cost almost nothing 

two countries away. People die from treatable 

diseases not because medicine does not exist 

but because they cannot find where it is affordable.

GeneriQ fixes the information gap.



\## Who We Help

\- UK patients overpaying for branded medicines 

&#x20; when generics exist

\- People in Nepal, India, Africa with no price 

&#x20; transparency

\- Anyone in the world who deserves to know 

&#x20; the cheapest legal option for their medicine



\## How It Works

1\. User searches medicine by name or ingredient

2\. We show all local pharmacy prices sorted 

&#x20;  cheapest first

3\. We show the same medicine price globally

4\. We link directly to where they can buy it
